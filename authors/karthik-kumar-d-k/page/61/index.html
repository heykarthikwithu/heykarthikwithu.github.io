Founder & Editor-in-Chief

Hi, I'm Karthik, the Founder & Editor-in-Chief of PeoplesBLOG and a software engineer with over a decade of experience in web technologies and digital publishing. I write about technology, travel, consumer products, social issues, and topics that shape our everyday lives.

As the Editor-in-Chief, I review and oversee all content published on PeoplesBLOG to ensure it is accurate, informative, and valuable to readers. I enjoy exploring new technologies, building web applications, and sharing practical insights based on research and real-world experience.

When I'm not coding or writing, you'll often find me planning motorcycle expeditions, capturing travel experiences, or working on new digital projects.
