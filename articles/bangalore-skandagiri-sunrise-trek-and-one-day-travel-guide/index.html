Bangalore to Skandagiri: Sunrise Trek and One-Day Travel Guide

By Karthik Kumar D K | Published: Nov 9, 2026 11:23 AM IST
By Karthik Kumar D K , 9 November 2026
Bangalore to Skandagiri: Sunrise Trek and One-Day Travel Guide

There is something special about watching the sunrise from the top of a mountain. The early morning chill, the quiet surroundings, the gradual appearance of sunlight and the clouds covering the hills create an experience that is difficult to get during an ordinary weekend. For people living in Bangalore, Skandagiri is one of the popular destinations to experience this without planning a long holiday.

Located near Chikkaballapur, approximately 60 km from Bangalore, Skandagiri is known for its night trekking experience and beautiful sunrise views. Also called Kalavara Durga, this hill attracts trekking enthusiasts, photographers and groups of friends looking for a quick getaway from the city.

If you are planning a Bangalore to Skandagiri trip, here is everything you need to know about the sunrise trek, travel options, timings, preparation and how to make the most of a one-day trip.

Where Is Skandagiri?

Skandagiri is a hill located near Kalavara village in the Chikkaballapur district of Karnataka. It stands at an elevation of approximately 1,450 metres above sea level and is known for its rocky trekking trail and panoramic views.

The hill is particularly popular for its sunrise trek. Many visitors begin climbing during the night so that they can reach the summit before sunrise. On clear mornings, the surrounding hills and valleys look beautiful as the first sunlight appears.

Skandagiri is also associated with the remains of an old hill fort, making it interesting for visitors who enjoy a combination of nature, history and outdoor activities.

Bangalore to Skandagiri Distance and Travel Options

The distance from Bangalore to Skandagiri is approximately 60 km, depending on your starting point and the route you take. The journey generally takes around 1.5 to 2 hours by road, depending on traffic.

The most convenient option is travelling by car or two-wheeler. You can take the Hyderabad Highway (NH 44) towards Chikkaballapur and follow the directions towards Skandagiri and Kalavara village.

By car or bike: This is the easiest option, especially if you are planning a night trek. You can leave Bangalore late at night, reach the base village and park in the designated parking area.

By public transport: You can travel from Bangalore to Chikkaballapur by bus or train, but reaching the actual trekking starting point may require an additional local taxi or auto. Public transport is less convenient for night trekking because of limited late-night connectivity.

If you are travelling with friends or family, sharing a car can make the journey more comfortable and economical.

Skandagiri Sunrise Trek: What to Expect

The main attraction of Skandagiri is its sunrise trek. Unlike destinations where visitors can drive close to the viewpoint, Skandagiri requires you to walk uphill to reach the summit.

The trek is approximately 4 km one way, although the exact distance can vary slightly depending on the route and starting point. The trail includes rocky sections, uneven surfaces and steep climbs in certain places.

Most visitors take around 2 to 3 hours to reach the top, depending on their fitness level and the pace of the group.

The night trek is particularly popular because it allows visitors to reach the summit before sunrise. Walking under the night sky with other trekking groups is an experience in itself.

As you climb higher, you will notice the temperature dropping. Once you reach the summit, you can take a break, enjoy the surroundings and wait for the sunrise.

On mornings when the weather conditions are favourable, the valleys below may be covered in a blanket of clouds. This is the famous sea-of-clouds view that makes Skandagiri a favourite among photographers and weekend travellers.

However, the cloud cover is not guaranteed. Weather conditions, visibility and the season can influence what you see.

How to Book Skandagiri Trek Tickets

Skandagiri is a regulated trekking destination, and visitors should check the official Karnataka Forest Department trekking reservation system before planning their visit.

Trekking access is subject to available slots, permitted timings and forest department rules. Do not assume that you can arrive at the base and start climbing without a reservation.

Book your trekking slot in advance, particularly if you are planning to visit on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday. Carry your booking confirmation and a valid identity document.

It is also important to follow the instructions provided for your selected trekking slot. Entry timings, group limits and other operational rules may change, so check the latest information before leaving Bangalore.

Best Time to Visit Skandagiri

Skandagiri can be visited during different parts of the year, but the overall experience depends heavily on weather conditions.

The post-monsoon and winter months are generally preferred by many trekking enthusiasts because of the cooler temperatures and pleasant conditions. October to February is a popular period for planning a sunrise trek.

During the monsoon, the trail can become slippery, and rain may affect visibility and trekking conditions. Extra caution is necessary when walking over rocks and uneven surfaces.

Summer treks can also be challenging because of warmer daytime temperatures. Since sunrise treks involve climbing during the night and early morning, carrying sufficient drinking water is still important.

If your main objective is to enjoy the famous cloud-covered sunrise, remember that nature does not follow a fixed schedule. A clear morning can be equally beautiful.

What to Carry for the Skandagiri Trek

You do not need expensive trekking equipment for Skandagiri, but carrying the right essentials can make the journey much more comfortable.

Here are a few things worth packing:

  • Comfortable trekking shoes: Choose shoes with a good grip because the trail has rocky and uneven sections.
  • Water bottle: Carry sufficient drinking water for the climb.
  • Light jacket: Early morning temperatures can be cold, especially near the summit.
  • Small backpack: Keep your belongings organised without carrying unnecessary weight.
  • Torch or headlamp: Useful during the night climb, although you should also follow the trek operator's or forest department's instructions.
  • Snacks: Carry light energy snacks if permitted under the current trekking rules.
  • Identity proof: Keep a valid ID along with your booking confirmation.
  • Power bank: Useful if you are using your phone for navigation or photography.

Avoid carrying plastic waste or leaving anything behind. Keep the trekking route clean and respect the natural surroundings.

Places to Visit Near Skandagiri

If you have some extra time after the trek, there are a few places around Chikkaballapur worth considering.

Nandi Hills: One of the most popular hill destinations near Bangalore, Nandi Hills offers scenic viewpoints, pleasant surroundings and historical attractions. It can be included in a separate trip or as an additional stop if you have sufficient time.

Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple: Located near Nandi Hills, this historic temple is known for its traditional architecture and peaceful surroundings. It is a good option for visitors interested in Karnataka's heritage.

Chikkaballapur: You can explore the town, stop for breakfast or lunch and experience a quieter side of the region before returning to Bangalore.

Avoid trying to cover too many destinations after a night trek. Fatigue can make additional travel uncomfortable, particularly if you are driving yourself.

How Much Does a Bangalore to Skandagiri Trip Cost?

The overall cost depends on your mode of transport, group size, food expenses and trekking booking charges.

For a rough budget, consider the following expenses:

  • Fuel or transportation: Depends on your vehicle and travel distance.
  • Trekking entry: Check the latest official booking charges.
  • Food and refreshments: Depends on your choice of restaurants.
  • Additional sightseeing: Depends on the places you decide to visit.

Travelling in a group and sharing transportation expenses can make the trip more affordable. Keep a little extra money for unexpected expenses.

Important Tips Before Planning Your Skandagiri Sunrise Trek

A little preparation can make a significant difference to your trekking experience.

  1. Book your trekking slot before travelling.
  2. Check the weather forecast and official trekking instructions.
  3. Avoid starting the trek without authorised entry.
  4. Wear shoes with a good grip.
  5. Do not rush during the climb or descent.
  6. Avoid carrying unnecessary luggage.
  7. Do not litter or disturb wildlife.
  8. If you are driving back after the trek, make sure you are sufficiently rested.

Skandagiri is manageable for many beginners with reasonable fitness, but it is not a completely effortless walk. The rocky trail and steep sections require attention and physical effort.

Is Skandagiri Worth Visiting from Bangalore?

If you enjoy trekking, nature and early morning mountain views, Skandagiri is certainly worth considering for a one-day trip from Bangalore.

The biggest attraction is the experience of climbing a hill before sunrise and watching the surrounding landscape gradually become visible in the morning light. You do not need an elaborate holiday plan or several days away from the city to enjoy this experience.

However, plan your visit responsibly. Check the official trekking availability, follow the entry instructions and prepare for the physical effort involved.

For Bangalore residents looking for a weekend escape, Skandagiri offers a good combination of adventure, nature and a memorable sunrise experience. Leave the city behind for a few hours, take the uphill trail and enjoy a different start to your day.
