Maddur Vada Recipe: How to Make Crispy Karnataka Style Maddur Vade at Home

If you have travelled between Bengaluru and Mysuru, chances are you have heard of Maddur Vada. This crispy, savoury snack is closely associated with Maddur, a town in Karnataka located between Bengaluru and Mysuru. Unlike the familiar doughnut-shaped South Indian medu vada, Maddur Vada is a flat, round fritter with no hole in the centre. It gets its characteristic crunch from rice flour and semolina, while finely chopped onions, curry leaves and green chillies provide its unmistakable flavour.
