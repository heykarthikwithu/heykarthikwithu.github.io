Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd – A Delicious Indian Breakfast Recipe

Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd – A Delicious Indian Breakfast Recipe

Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd is a comforting and filling Indian meal that brings together the goodness of whole wheat flour, spiced potatoes and fresh curd. Popular in North Indian households, particularly Punjab, this traditional stuffed paratha has become a favourite across India. Whether served for breakfast, brunch or lunch, it is a satisfying homemade dish that can be prepared with everyday kitchen ingredients.

The best part of making Aloo Paratha at home is that you can adjust the spices, stuffing and amount of ghee according to your family's preferences. The potato filling is flavoured with green chillies, cumin, coriander leaves and a few everyday Indian spices. Each paratha is rolled with the stuffing inside and cooked on a hot tawa until both sides develop beautiful golden-brown spots.

Serving these hot parathas with chilled homemade curd makes the meal even more enjoyable. The mild, creamy taste of fresh curd balances the spices in the potato filling. You can also add a little pickle or homemade butter to complete the traditional combination.

If you are looking for an easy Indian breakfast recipe that is filling, delicious and suitable for the entire family, this Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd recipe is worth trying.

Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd – Recipe Information

  • Preparation Time: 25 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 25 minutes
  • Total Time: 50 minutes
  • Servings: 3–4 people
  • Cuisine: Indian, North Indian
  • Course: Breakfast / Lunch
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Diet: Vegetarian

Ingredients Required for Aloo Paratha

The following ingredients are sufficient to prepare approximately 6 medium-sized stuffed aloo parathas.

For the Paratha Dough

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon oil or ghee
  • Water as required for kneading
  • Extra wheat flour for rolling

For the Potato Stuffing

  • 4 medium-sized potatoes
  • 2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds or cumin powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon amchur (dry mango powder)
  • ½ teaspoon coriander powder
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped coriander leaves
  • Salt as required

For Cooking

  • 3–4 tablespoons ghee or oil, as required

For Homemade Curd

  • 500 ml full-fat milk
  • 1–2 teaspoons plain curd (as a starter)

You can use regular toned milk if you prefer lighter curd. Full-fat milk generally produces thicker and creamier homemade curd.

How to Make Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd

Follow these simple steps to prepare the potato stuffing, knead the dough, make the parathas and set fresh homemade curd.

Step 1: Prepare the Potatoes

Wash the potatoes thoroughly and pressure cook them with sufficient water until they become soft. Depending on the size of the potatoes and pressure cooker, this usually takes around 3–4 whistles.

Allow the pressure to release naturally. Remove the potatoes and let them cool enough to handle. Peel the skin and mash them thoroughly using a potato masher or fork.

Make sure there are no large potato pieces or lumps. A smooth and evenly mashed potato mixture makes it easier to roll the stuffed parathas without breaking the dough.

Step 2: Prepare the Aloo Stuffing

Take the mashed potatoes in a large mixing bowl. Add finely chopped green chillies, grated ginger, cumin, red chilli powder, garam masala, amchur powder, coriander powder and salt.

Add the chopped coriander leaves and mix everything well.

Taste the stuffing and adjust the salt and spices according to your preference. The filling should have a balanced combination of mild heat, earthy spices and a slight tanginess.

Keep the prepared potato stuffing aside.

Step 3: Knead the Wheat Flour Dough

Take whole wheat flour in another large bowl. Add salt and one teaspoon of oil or ghee.

Gradually add water and start kneading. Prepare a soft, smooth and pliable dough. Avoid adding too much water at once, as the dough can become sticky.

Knead for approximately 5–7 minutes until the texture becomes smooth.

Cover the dough with a clean, damp cloth or a lid and allow it to rest for 20 minutes. Resting helps make the dough easier to roll and keeps the parathas soft.

Step 4: Stuff and Roll the Parathas

Divide the prepared dough into six equal portions and shape them into small balls.

Take one dough ball and flatten it slightly using your fingers. Dust it with a little dry wheat flour and roll it into a small circle.

Place a generous portion of the prepared potato stuffing in the centre. Bring the edges of the dough together over the stuffing and seal them firmly.

Gently flatten the stuffed dough ball. Dust it with dry flour and roll it carefully into a medium-sized round paratha.

Use gentle and even pressure while rolling. Applying too much pressure in one place can cause the potato stuffing to come out.

Repeat the same process with the remaining dough balls and potato filling.

Step 5: Cook the Aloo Parathas on a Tawa

Heat a tawa or flat griddle over medium heat.

Place one rolled paratha on the hot tawa. Cook for a few seconds until small bubbles begin to appear on the surface. Flip it and cook the other side briefly.

Apply a little ghee or oil on both sides. Continue cooking while gently pressing the edges with a spatula.

Flip occasionally until both sides develop golden-brown spots and the paratha is cooked properly.

The outside should have a lightly crisp texture, while the inside should remain soft and filled with flavourful potato stuffing.

Remove the cooked paratha and keep it in a covered container to retain its warmth.

Repeat the process for the remaining parathas.

How to Make Homemade Curd

Fresh homemade curd is an excellent accompaniment to hot aloo parathas. Instead of using packaged curd, you can prepare creamy curd at home with just two ingredients.

Step 1: Boil the Milk

Pour 500 ml of milk into a clean saucepan and bring it to a boil over medium heat.

Stir occasionally to prevent the milk from sticking to the bottom. Once it boils, switch off the flame.

Step 2: Allow the Milk to Cool

Let the boiled milk cool until it becomes lukewarm. It should feel comfortably warm to the touch, not hot.

Adding the curd starter while the milk is too hot can affect the setting process.

Step 3: Add the Curd Starter

Take 1–2 teaspoons of plain curd in a clean bowl. Add a little lukewarm milk and mix until smooth.

Pour this mixture into the remaining milk and stir gently.

Step 4: Allow the Curd to Set

Transfer the milk into a clean container and cover it with a lid.

Keep it undisturbed in a warm place for approximately 6–8 hours. During colder weather, it may take longer to set.

Once the curd becomes firm, refrigerate it for a few hours before serving.

Your fresh homemade curd is now ready.

How to Serve Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd

Serve the aloo parathas hot, directly from the tawa, with a bowl of chilled homemade curd.

You can add a small amount of roasted cumin powder and a pinch of black salt to the curd for additional flavour.

For a more traditional Punjabi-style meal, serve the parathas with:

  • Fresh homemade curd
  • Mango or lemon pickle
  • Homemade white butter
  • Sliced onions
  • Green chilli

The combination of hot, spiced potato parathas and cool, creamy curd makes a satisfying breakfast or lunch.

Tips for Making Perfect Aloo Parathas

A few simple precautions can make a noticeable difference when preparing stuffed parathas at home.

  1. Use completely cooled potatoes: Warm potatoes can sometimes make the stuffing moist and difficult to handle.
  2. Mash the potatoes properly: Avoid large pieces and lumps, as they can tear the dough while rolling.
  3. Keep the stuffing dry: Excess moisture in the potato mixture can make the parathas difficult to roll.
  4. Rest the dough: Allowing the dough to rest for around 20 minutes helps make it softer and easier to work with.
  5. Avoid overfilling: Use enough potato stuffing for a generous filling, but leave sufficient dough to seal it properly.
  6. Roll gently: Apply even pressure instead of pressing hard in one spot.
  7. Maintain medium heat: Cooking on medium heat allows the paratha to cook properly inside without burning the outside.
  8. Use ghee for flavour: Ghee gives the parathas a pleasant aroma and a traditional taste.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I prepare aloo paratha stuffing in advance?

Yes. You can prepare the potato stuffing a few hours ahead and store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator. Bring it closer to room temperature before preparing the parathas.

2. Why does potato stuffing come out while rolling?

This usually happens when the stuffing contains lumps or excess moisture, the dough is not sealed properly, or too much pressure is applied while rolling. Use smooth, dry potato stuffing and roll gently.

3. Can I make aloo paratha without ghee?

Yes. You can use regular cooking oil instead of ghee. However, ghee gives the parathas a richer aroma and traditional flavour.

4. How long does homemade curd take to set?

Homemade curd generally takes around 6–8 hours to set in a warm environment. The time may increase during colder weather.

5. Can I use leftover boiled potatoes for aloo paratha?

Yes. Leftover boiled potatoes can be used as long as they have been stored properly and are fresh. Mash them well and mix with the spices before stuffing.

Final Thoughts

Aloo Paratha with Homemade Curd is a simple yet satisfying Indian recipe that is perfect for a homemade breakfast or lunch. With soft wheat dough, flavourful potato stuffing and freshly prepared curd, this combination brings together familiar ingredients to create a delicious meal.

You do not need complicated cooking techniques or expensive ingredients to prepare this dish. A little preparation, properly mashed potatoes and careful cooking on a tawa are enough to make delicious stuffed parathas at home.

Try this easy Aloo Paratha recipe with fresh homemade curd and enjoy a comforting Indian meal with your family.
