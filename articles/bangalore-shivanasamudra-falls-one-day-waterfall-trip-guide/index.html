Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls: One-Day Waterfall Trip Guide

By Karthik Kumar D K | Published: Oct 5, 2026 8:03 AM IST
By Karthik Kumar D K , 5 October 2026
Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls: One-Day Waterfall Trip Guide

If you are looking for a one-day road trip from Bangalore where you can spend a few hours around a waterfall without planning a long vacation, Shivanasamudra Falls is a good option.

Located around 130 km from Bangalore, Shivanasamudra is formed by the Kaveri River and is known for its two major waterfalls, Gaganachukki and Barachukki. The drive takes you away from the city, through highways, smaller towns and countryside roads before reaching the waterfall region.

For people planning a Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls one-day trip, the destination can be covered comfortably in a day if you start early.

The important thing to remember is that Shivanasamudra is not just one waterfall. Gaganachukki and Barachukki are separate viewpoints, and both are worth visiting.

Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls Distance

The Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls distance is approximately 120–135 km, depending on where you start in Bangalore and the route you take.

Karnataka Tourism lists Shivanasamudra at about 133 km from Bengaluru. The journey can take around 3 to 4 hours depending on traffic, road conditions and the number of stops you make.

If you are travelling on a weekend, leaving Bangalore early is strongly recommended.

A start around 5:30 AM to 6:00 AM gives you a better chance of getting out of the city before traffic becomes heavy. It also leaves enough time to visit both waterfalls without rushing.

Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls Route

There are two main routes from Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls, and both can be used for a one-day road trip.

The more commonly used route is:

Bangalore → Ramanagara → Maddur → Malavalli → Shivanasamudra

This route takes you through Ramanagara and Maddur before continuing towards Malavalli and Shivanasamudra. It is a practical option if you are travelling from the southern or central parts of Bangalore.

The second route is:

Bangalore → Kanakapura → Malavalli → Shivanasamudra

The Kanakapura route is another popular option, particularly for people who prefer the road through Kanakapura and the surrounding countryside.

Which route works better for you can depend on where you are starting from in Bangalore and the traffic conditions on the day of your trip. Check live navigation before leaving, especially if you are travelling on a weekend or during a holiday.

If you are travelling by car or motorcycle, road travel is the most convenient way to reach Shivanasamudra.

Gaganachukki and Barachukki Falls

This is the most important part of planning a Shivanasamudra trip.

The Kaveri River divides around Shivanasamudra and forms two waterfalls — Gaganachukki and Barachukki.

The two viewing areas are roughly 15 km apart, so don't assume that reaching one viewpoint means you have covered Shivanasamudra completely.

Gaganachukki Falls

Gaganachukki is the more dramatic-looking waterfall, particularly when the Kaveri is carrying a lot of water.

The water drops through a rocky gorge, creating a powerful view from the designated viewing area. The sound of the waterfall and the surrounding greenery make it a good place to spend some time rather than simply stopping for photographs.

There are viewpoints from where you can safely see the falls. Avoid trying to climb down rocky areas or cross barriers for better photographs.

Barachukki Falls

Barachukki offers a different view of the Kaveri.

The waterfall spreads across the rocky landscape and the approach has viewing areas from which you can see the water flowing down the gorge.

The two waterfalls are close enough to cover in the same trip but far enough apart that you should include travel time between them in your itinerary.

If you have only a few hours, don't spend the entire morning at the first waterfall. Keep enough time for Barachukki as well.

Best Time to Visit Shivanasamudra Falls

If your main reason for visiting Shivanasamudra is the waterfall, the monsoon and post-monsoon months are generally the most rewarding.

During the monsoon, the Kaveri carries more water and both Gaganachukki and Barachukki look much more powerful. The surrounding landscape also becomes greener.

July to October is generally a good period for seeing strong water flow, while the post-monsoon months can offer a better balance between water levels and comfortable weather. Karnataka Tourism also recommends Shivanasamudra particularly during and after the monsoon.

However, there is a trade-off.

Weekends during the monsoon can be crowded, and wet rocks around waterfalls can become extremely slippery. Water activities can also be restricted depending on the conditions.

If you want a quieter experience, a weekday morning is a much better option.

Shivanasamudra Falls Timings

The timings can vary between the two locations.

Karnataka Tourism currently lists Barachukki Falls as open from 8 AM to 5 PM, while the Gaganachukki viewpoint is accessible during the daytime.

For a one-day trip from Bangalore, reaching the area around 9 AM is a good target.

There is little advantage in reaching very late in the afternoon because you still have the return journey to Bangalore.

Things to Do at Shivanasamudra

A Shivanasamudra trip doesn't need a long list of activities.

The main attraction is, of course, the waterfalls.

You can:

  • Visit both Gaganachukki and Barachukki
  • Spend time at the viewpoints
  • Take photographs of the waterfalls and surrounding landscape
  • Explore the nearby temples
  • Enjoy the countryside drive
  • Visit nearby attractions if you have enough time

The area is particularly good for people who simply want to spend a few hours away from Bangalore.

You don't need to turn the trip into a packed sightseeing schedule.

Visit the Temples at Shivanasamudra

Shivanasamudra is also more than just a waterfall destination.

The island area has temples including the Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple and Sri Someshwara Temple.

If you are interested in Karnataka's history and religious sites, spending some time at the temples can add another dimension to the trip.

This is also one reason why Shivanasamudra works well as a day trip. You get a combination of nature, river views and heritage without having to travel to another destination.

Places to Visit Near Shivanasamudra

If you start early and don't mind extending the day, you can consider adding one nearby destination.

Talakadu is one of the more practical options. It is known for its temples and the unusual sandy landscape along the Kaveri. Karnataka Tourism places Talakadu around 28 km from Shivanasamudra.

Somanathapura is another interesting option for people who enjoy history and architecture. The famous Hoysala temple is around the broader Shivanasamudra region and can be considered if you want to combine the waterfall trip with a heritage visit.

You can also consider Bheemeshwari if nature and outdoor activities are more important to you.

But don't try to cover everything in one day.

A common mistake with Bangalore road trips is creating an itinerary with five or six destinations and then spending more time inside the car than at the places you actually wanted to visit.

For Shivanasamudra, visiting both waterfalls should be the priority.

Food on the Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Route

There are small restaurants, local eateries and shops along the route, but don't expect the same choice you get in Bangalore.

A practical approach is to have breakfast somewhere along the route and carry some water and snacks.

There are shops around the tourist areas, but carrying basic refreshments is still a good idea, especially if you are travelling with children.

For lunch, you can eat at a local restaurant around the route back towards Malavalli or stop at a suitable place depending on your return route.

If you are travelling on a weekend, don't wait until you are very hungry before looking for lunch. The more popular local restaurants can get busy.

Can You Swim at Shivanasamudra Falls?

This is one area where you should not take chances.

The waterfall may look inviting, particularly during hot weather, but the Kaveri has strong currents, slippery rocks and unpredictable water conditions.

Do not enter restricted water areas or attempt to climb down the rocks around the falls.

Karnataka Tourism warns visitors about the dangers around the waterfalls and notes that swimming is prohibited. There are also no lifeguards stationed at the falls.

During the monsoon, the risk is even higher because of the increased water flow.

You can enjoy the falls perfectly well from the designated viewpoints.

What to Carry for a Shivanasamudra Trip

You don't need to carry a large travel bag for a one-day trip.

Keep it simple:

  • Drinking water
  • Snacks
  • Comfortable shoes
  • Sunglasses
  • Sunscreen
  • Cap or hat
  • Power bank
  • Basic first-aid supplies
  • Rain protection during the monsoon
  • Extra clothes if you are travelling with children

Good footwear is particularly important.

Avoid slippers or footwear with poor grip if you are visiting during the rainy season. Wet surfaces around waterfalls can be much more slippery than they appear.

Also carry some cash for small local expenses.

A practical itinerary can look like this:

11:15 AM–1:00 PMExplore Barachukki and spend time at the viewpoints

The timings are only a guide. If you are travelling with family or children, keep the schedule flexible.

The most important thing is to start the return journey while there is still plenty of daylight.

Is Shivanasamudra Good for a Family Trip?

Yes.

The relatively manageable distance from Bangalore makes Shivanasamudra suitable for a family day trip, provided you are comfortable with spending several hours on the road.

Families with young children should keep them close to the designated viewing areas and avoid slippery or unprotected sections.

If elderly family members are travelling with you, plan the visit around the accessible viewpoints rather than trying to cover every walking path.

A family trip can also be made more relaxed by avoiding weekends and public holidays.

Is Shivanasamudra Worth the Trip from Bangalore?

For a one-day road trip, Shivanasamudra is worth considering, particularly after the monsoon.

It is close enough to Bangalore to complete in a day, but the landscape feels very different from the city. The Kaveri River, rocky gorge, waterfalls and surrounding greenery give you enough of a change of scenery without requiring a long holiday.

The best way to plan the trip is to keep it simple.

Leave Bangalore early, reach Gaganachukki, visit Barachukki, have lunch, spend some quiet time around the area and start the return journey before evening.

If you want to add Talakadu, Somanathapura or another nearby destination, do it only if you have enough time.

The Bangalore to Shivanasamudra Falls road trip is not about ticking off as many tourist spots as possible. It is about getting out of Bangalore for a day, seeing the Kaveri in one of its most dramatic settings and returning home without making the trip unnecessarily complicated.

That is what makes Shivanasamudra a good weekend escape from Bangalore.
